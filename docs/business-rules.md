# Business Rules

## Main Business Entities

- `User`: system account for Admin or Staff.
- `Vehicle`: fleet asset used for bookings.
- `Driver`: person assigned to bookings.
- `Customer`: person or organization receiving travel service.
- `Booking`: reservation connecting customer, vehicle, driver, and time window.
- `OvernightStay`: overnight segment attached to a booking.
- `Expense`: operational cost associated with booking, driver, vehicle, or overnight stay.
- `AuditLog`: immutable record of important system actions.
- `SystemSetting`: configurable operational setting.
- `Notification`: message or alert generated by system events.

## Initial Statuses

### Booking

- `DRAFT`
- `CONFIRMED`
- `IN_PROGRESS`
- `COMPLETED`
- `CANCELLED`

### Vehicle

- `AVAILABLE`
- `BOOKED`
- `MAINTENANCE`
- `OUT_OF_SERVICE`
- `INACTIVE`

### Driver

- `AVAILABLE`
- `ASSIGNED`
- `ON_LEAVE`
- `INACTIVE`

## Booking Rules

- Booking start time must be before end time.
- Booking availability uses half-open intervals: `[startAt, endAt)`.
- A conflict exists when `newStart < existingEnd` and `newEnd > existingStart`.
- Voucher numbers must be unique.
- A booking customer must be active and not soft deleted.
- Vehicles under maintenance, out of service, inactive, or soft deleted must not be assigned to new or updated bookings.
- Inactive or soft-deleted drivers must not be assigned to new or updated bookings.
- Completed bookings are read-only for Staff users.
- Cancelled bookings cannot move to `IN_PROGRESS` or `COMPLETED`.
- Only Admin users can perform exceptional reopen/status changes.
- Cancelled bookings should not reserve vehicles or drivers.
- Critical booking writes must be transactional.
- Active bookings must not overlap for the same vehicle.
- Active bookings must not overlap for the same driver.
- Cancelled bookings do not block availability.
- Booking create and update operations must recheck availability inside the database transaction.
- PostgreSQL exclusion constraints using `btree_gist` and `tstzrange` provide the database-level protection for vehicle and driver overlap.
- Conflict checks use the persisted availability window, not only the visible trip start and end times.
- For normal city and outside-city bookings, the availability window equals `startAt` to `endAt`.
- For overnight bookings, the availability window extends by configurable pre-trip and post-trip buffer hours.

### Phase 5 Status Transitions

Staff users can move:

- `DRAFT` to `CONFIRMED` or `CANCELLED`.
- `CONFIRMED` to `IN_PROGRESS` or `CANCELLED`.
- `IN_PROGRESS` to `COMPLETED` or `CANCELLED`.

Admin users can additionally reopen or reverse operational statuses where the transition is explicitly allowed by the status transition service.

## Overnight Stay Rules

- An overnight stay must belong to exactly one booking.
- `CITY` bookings must not contain overnight stay data and do not calculate overnight costs.
- `OUTSIDE_CITY` bookings require a destination and may later support optional overnight details.
- `OVERNIGHT` bookings require destination, accommodation name, check-in date, and check-out date.
- Overnight check-out date must be after check-in date.
- Nights count is calculated from the date-only check-in/check-out range.
- Driver overnight cost is calculated as `nightsCount * driverDailyRate`.
- The driver daily rate is stored on the overnight stay at booking time so historical totals are preserved.
- Staff users use the configured driver/default rate and cannot override overnight rate or cost.
- Admin users can override overnight rate or total cost only with a required reason.
- Overnight cost overrides must be written to the audit log.
- Overnight bookings visibly mark the booking as an overnight booking in lists and details.
- Overnight stay ranges must be valid date ranges.
- Overnight stays should be visible in booking details and reports.
- Default overnight driver rate, pre-trip buffer hours, post-trip buffer hours, currency, and operating timezone are configurable through system settings.

## Soft Deletion Rules

- Important business records should use soft deletion when deletion would affect reporting or audit history.
- Soft-deleted records should be hidden from normal operational lists.
- Customers are soft deleted so historical booking history remains intact.
- Bookings are not permanently deleted in normal operations.
- Vehicle and driver deletion actions must soft-delete records and set them inactive.
- Inactive or out-of-service vehicles must not be selectable for future bookings.
- Inactive drivers must not be assignable to future bookings.

## Timezone Rules

- Store timestamps in UTC.
- Display timestamps using the configured local timezone.
- Never infer timezone rules from the client browser alone for official reports.

## Audit Rules

- Record actor, action, entity type, entity id, timestamp, and metadata.
- Audit logs must not be editable through normal application flows.

## Dashboard And Timeline Rules

- Dashboard statistics must be calculated with backend aggregate queries.
- The browser must not load all bookings to calculate operational summary cards.
- Timeline bookings are grouped by vehicle row and mapped from persisted booking availability windows.
- Timeline overlap display must match the same `[availabilityStartAt, availabilityEndAt)` period used by conflict prevention.
- Staff users may view operational dashboard data but must not receive restricted financial or administrative statistics.
- Long operational lists should be limited or paginated; the vehicle timeline should render the active fleet window rather than the full booking history.
- Cancelled bookings may be displayed with a distinct visual state but must not be counted as availability-blocking active work.

## Report And Export Rules

- Reports must be generated from backend-validated filters.
- Report exports must require authentication.
- Staff users may access operational reports but not financial reports.
- Financial reports include booking expenses and overnight driver cost reports.
- Report date ranges must be bounded to avoid very large synchronous exports.
- Excel exports must escape user-provided values that could be interpreted as spreadsheet formulas.
- PDF exports must use an Arabic-capable font when available through `REPORT_ARABIC_FONT_PATH` or known deployment font paths.
- Export responses must not expose raw filesystem paths.
- Significant report exports must create audit-log records.
